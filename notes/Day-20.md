# Day 20

## What did I learn today?

Today I learned what embeddings are and why they are one of the core building blocks of modern AI applications. I understood that embeddings represent the semantic meaning of text as high-dimensional vectors, allowing computers to compare pieces of text based on meaning rather than exact words.

## Biggest insight today

The biggest insight was realizing the difference between keyword search and semantic search. Keyword search looks for exact words, while semantic search compares the meanings of sentences. This allows an AI to retrieve relevant information even when different words or synonyms are used.

## What surprised me?

I was surprised that an embedding is just a list of numbers. Those numbers don't contain the original sentence but instead represent its position in a high-dimensional mathematical space where similar meanings are located close together.

## What was challenging?

Understanding high-dimensional vector spaces was initially difficult because they can't be visualized like 2D or 3D graphs. Thinking of embeddings as points on a huge map of meaning made the concept much easier to understand.

## One thing I still want to learn

I want to learn how embedding vectors are actually generated and how AI systems measure similarity between them. I'm also curious about how embeddings are used to retrieve documents in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems.

## Summary

Today's lesson was entirely conceptual and laid the foundation for semantic search and RAG. Although I didn't write any code, I now understand why embeddings are essential for helping AI systems search and retrieve information based on meaning instead of exact keyword matches.
